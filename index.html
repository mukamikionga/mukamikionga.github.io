About Mukami

A clinician who sees what families cannot say

Families are breaking quietly. Not with drama. Not with conflict anyone can see. With distance. With conversations that never happen. With children who have stopped bringing the real things home.

I am a clinical psychologist and family reconciliation specialist with over a decade of experience sitting with families who love each other deeply but cannot reach each other. My work sits at the intersection of evidence-based psychology and the African family experience.

I speak to corporate teams, leadership conferences, and staff wellness programmes on the one topic that affects every person in every room: what is happening inside their family, and what to do about it.

The Signature Keynote

The Empty Template

Every parent walks into parenthood carrying a template they did not choose.

The family they grew up in. The silence they inherited. The patterns they swore they would never repeat. The wounds they did not know were wounds until they watched themselves pass them on.

Most parents are not failing because they do not love their children enough. They are failing because nobody gave them a different template. And without awareness, we build our families from the only blueprint we have ever seen.

This keynote is about what happens when that template runs out. And what it takes — deliberately, courageously, and with clinical precision — to build a new one.

Available as a keynote, half-day workshop, or staff wellness session.

Our Mission

Laja
Trust

Laja — a Yoruba word meaning to reconcile.

Healed families.
Changed generations.

Laja Trust walks alongside adolescents, youth and families, guiding them from awareness through healing to empowerment, so that the cycles of the past do not become the foundations of the future.

We began in 2018 working with adolescents. Real progress was made, until they went home. Nothing had changed. That observation shaped everything. A young person cannot be healed in isolation from the family system they live inside.

Hope
Most families arrive believing it is too late. We carry hope on their behalf, until they are able to carry it themselves.
Faith
The Word of God is our foundation. Truth is what produces lasting freedom. We draw on both biblical wisdom and evidence-based psychology, because these are not in conflict.
Love
If Christ is the foundation, love is the method. The love that sees people fully, refuses to shame them, and holds firm in the belief that they can be changed.
Adolescents
Our primary focus. Young people navigating family disconnection, identity and patterns they did not choose.
Youth
Young people moving into adulthood, building their own foundations for the first time.
Families
Specifically parents, whose own healing makes a young person's growth sustainable at home.
